Finds esp-usbip-bridges advertising _hil-bridge._tcp over mDNS and confirms each one with GET /api/capabilities (the TXT record is a hint; the endpoint is the truth). Results are proposals: nothing is added, removed or disabled until you press Apply on one. A bridge you can't see here (e.g. across Tailscale, where multicast does not go) can be added by address. API: GET /v1/bridges/discover, POST /v1/bridges/probe, POST /v1/bridges/apply.
